VIT Academic Policies, FFCS, 9-Pointer Attendance Exemption & Grading System:

1. 9-Pointer Attendance Exemption Policy (9-Pointer Rule / 9ptr):
- Under VIT academic regulations, undergraduate and postgraduate students who achieve and maintain a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 9.00 or higher ('9-pointers') enjoy 100% attendance flexibility.
- Key benefits:
  * Complete exemption from the mandatory 75% minimum attendance requirement in all registered theory courses.
  * 9-pointer students cannot be debarred from CAT exams or FAT exams due to low attendance.
  * They have the liberty to engage in self-study, research projects, hackathons, and technical clubs during class hours without attendance penalties.
  * Note: Lab practical sessions, project reviews, and internal assessments (CATs/quizzes) must still be attended.

2. Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS):
- FFCS is VIT's hallmark academic framework allowing students to craft their own personalized timetable.
- Features:
  * Choice of Faculty: Select preferred professors based on teaching style and reviews.
  * Choice of Timings: Slot-based scheduling offering Morning Theory / Afternoon Lab or Afternoon Theory / Morning Lab.
  * Pace of Learning: Fast-track degree completion (up to 27 credits per semester) or relaxed pace (minimum 16 credits per semester).
  * Major & Minor Degrees: Enroll in interdisciplinary courses to earn Minor specialization in AI, Cyber Security, Finance, etc.
  * Add/Drop Period: Opportunity to modify courses during the first week of the semester.

3. Evaluation and Grading Scheme:
- Continuous Assessment:
  * CAT-1 (Continuous Assessment Test 1): 15% weightage (Conducted for 50 marks, 1.5 hours).
  * CAT-2 (Continuous Assessment Test 2): 15% weightage (Conducted for 50 marks, 1.5 hours).
  * Digital Assignments (DA) / Quizzes: 30% weightage (Online quizzes on VTOP, coding assignments, case studies).
  * FAT (Final Assessment Test): 40% weightage (Comprehensive 100 marks exam, 3 hours). Minimum 40/100 required in FAT to pass the course.
- Relative Grading Curve:
  * S Grade: 10 Grade Points (Top 5-10% of class, typically >= 90 depending on standard deviation).
  * A Grade: 9 Grade Points.
  * B Grade: 8 Grade Points.
  * C Grade: 7 Grade Points.
  * D Grade: 6 Grade Points.
  * E Grade: 5 Grade Points (Passing grade).
  * F Grade: 0 Grade Points (Fail - re-registration required).
  * N Grade: Debarred due to low attendance (< 75% attendance without 9-pointer status).
