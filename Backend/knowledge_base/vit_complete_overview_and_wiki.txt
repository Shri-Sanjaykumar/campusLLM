Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) - Comprehensive University Profile:

Overview & History:
Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is a premier private deemed university located in Katpadi, Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India. 
Founded in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College by Dr. G. Viswanathan, a former parliamentarian and minister in the Government of Tamil Nadu, the institution was granted deemed-to-be-university status by the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD) in 2001. 
VIT has major campuses across India:
1. VIT Vellore (Main Campus, Tamil Nadu - 372 acres eco-friendly campus)
2. VIT Chennai (Vandalur-Kelambakkam Road, Tamil Nadu)
3. VIT-AP (Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh)
4. VIT Bhopal (Kothrikalan, Sehore, Madhya Pradesh)
5. VIT Bangalore (Upcoming campus)

Rankings & Accreditation:
- Ranked consistently among the top 10 engineering institutions in India by NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework).
- Awarded Institution of Eminence (IoE) status by the Government of India.
- Accredited with the highest grade 'A++' by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) in the 4th cycle.
- Ranked in the top 601-700 bracket globally by the QS World University Rankings and Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings.
- ABET (Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology) accredited for multiple engineering programs.

Schools at VIT Vellore:
- SCOPE (School of Computer Science and Engineering)
- SITE (School of Information Technology and Engineering)
- SENSE (School of Electronics Engineering)
- SELECT (School of Electrical Engineering)
- SMEC (School of Mechanical Engineering)
- SCHEME (School of Chemical Engineering)
- SAS (School of Advanced Sciences - Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
- SSL (School of Social Sciences and Languages)
- VSMS (VIT School of Management)
- VAIAL (VIT School of Agricultural Innovations and Advanced Learning)
- V-SPARC (VIT School of Planning and Architecture)

Academics & Festivals:
- Riviera: The annual 4-day international sports and cultural festival of VIT Vellore held in February, featuring 40,000+ students, pro-shows, and competitive events.
- graVITas: The annual international techno-management festival of VIT Vellore held in September/October with 24-hour hackathons and robotics challenges.
- Vibrance: The annual cultural festival of VIT Chennai held in February/March.
