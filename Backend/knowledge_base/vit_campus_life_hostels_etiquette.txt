VIT Campus Life, Hostels, VTOP Leave System, and 13 Campus Etiquette Rules:

1. The 13 Golden Rules of VIT Campus Etiquette:
1. Please keep left while walking in corridors and climbing stairs.
2. Do not crowd in corridors and walkways; give way to people passing by.
3. Wait till people get out before entering an elevator.
4. Do not throw papers and waste around; use waste bins.
5. Be respectful, kind, and helpful to visitors, teachers, and students.
6. Maintain silence in libraries, classrooms, and study areas.
7. Keep mobiles on silent mode in class; avoid loud conversations and music indoors.
8. Be punctual; respect everyone's time.
9. Dress appropriately, neatly, and adhere to the university dress code.
10. Keep campus pristine: avoid scribbling on walls, desks, and damaging facilities. Avoid plucking flowers and damaging plants.
11. Do not jump queues at food courts, stores, and library counters.
12. Use water and electricity wisely: turn off lights, ACs, and fans when not needed.
13. Report any suspicious or unsafe activity to authorities promptly.

2. Hostel Facilities & Room Types:
- Men's Hostels (MH: Blocks A to T) & Ladies Hostels (LH: Blocks A to J).
- Room Categories: Single bed, 2-bed, 3-bed, 4-bed, and 6-bed with AC and Non-AC options.
- Mess Options: Special Mess (multi-cuisine), North Indian Mess, South Indian Mess, Vegetarian Mess, and Non-Vegetarian Mess.
- Wi-Fi high-speed internet, 24x7 power backup, gymnasiums, indoor sports badminton courts, swimming pool.

3. VTOP Proctor Leave & Hostel Curfew:
- Curfew Hours:
  * Non-night permission: Return by 8:30 PM (LH) / 9:00 PM (MH).
  * Library permission: Late night study permitted up to 11:00 PM with ID scan.
- Leave Application:
  * Applied through the VTOP student portal (Home leave, Outing, Weekend leave, Medical leave).
  * Requires Proctor approval + automated OTP/consent verification from registered parent mobile number for overnight home leaves.
