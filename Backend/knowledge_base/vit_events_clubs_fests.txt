Clubs, Chapters, Cultural Festivals, and Placements at VIT:

1. Riviera (International Cultural & Sports Festival):
- Riviera is VIT Vellore's flagship 4-day annual international cultural and sports fest, held every year in February.
- Certified with ISO 9001:2008 quality management standard.
- Attracts over 40,000+ students from 700+ colleges across India and abroad.
- Highlights: Pro-Shows featuring international artists and Bollywood singers (Arijit Singh, Armaan Malik, Nucleya, Andrea Jeremiah), marathon, fashion shows (Words Worth), dance battles, dramatic events, and 30+ sports tournaments.

2. graVITas (International Techno-Management Festival):
- Annual 3-day technical festival held in September/October.
- Features major 24-hour and 36-hour hackathons, robotics challenges, coding marathons, aerospace competitions, management conclaves, and industry keynote lectures.

3. Student Clubs & Professional Chapters:
- Over 130 active student clubs and international chapters:
  * Technical Chapters: IEEE, ACM, CSI, ASME, SAE (automotive formula student teams: Team Pravega, Team Ojas), Developer Student Clubs (GDSC).
  * Cultural & Literary: Dance Club, Music Club, Photography Club (VIT Spartan), English/Hindi/Tamil Literary Societies, Nature Club, Youth Red Cross.

4. Placements & Career Highlights:
- Career Development Centre (CDC) manages placements.
- Over 900+ recruiting companies visit annually (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Intel, Qualcomm, Cisco, Deloitte, TCS, Infosys).
- Super Dream Offers: Packages above ₹10 LPA (Highest CTC exceeding ₹1.02 Crore international).
- Over 12,000+ job offers generated annually.
